== Bringing existing accounts along

Users who already sign in with a password do not have to be asked to switch.
From iOS 18, xref:upgradeToPasskey.adoc[] creates a passkey for them in the background, right after a
password sign-in: no upsell screen, no extra tap, and their password keeps working.
Both password sign-ins take care of it through their `upgradingToPasskey` parameter —
xref:loginWithPassword.adoc[] and xref:loginWithRequest.adoc[] with `.Password`.
